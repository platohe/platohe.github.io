Verify a DataStudio Run Card

offline

This page runs entirely in your browser, from this file, with no network access. It checks that a card was not edited after it was written, and — if you also supply an evidence bundle — that the inputs it describes are the ones you have.

Drop a .dscard.json here

Add an evidence bundle too, if you have one, to check the input digests.

Drop a card to verify it.
Structural verification proves the card was not edited in transit. It does not prove the data or the result: that needs the committed goldens the card names. A signature means signed by that install, which is integrity, not identity.
